Blantyre Route Finder

Limbe Market to Blantyre Market · 22 places · 32 roads · Dijkstra and minimum spanning tree

Dry run, step by step

Each step settles the unvisited place with the smallest tentative distance, then updates its neighbours. The run stops once the destination is settled.

Places and distances come from our hand-drawn graph of Blantyre city, from Limbe Market (left) to Blantyre Market (right). Distances are approximate road distances in kilometres. The layout follows the drawing, not an exact map.

This page runs the same algorithms as the C++ program in the repository (src/graph.hpp), written in JavaScript so it works in any browser.
